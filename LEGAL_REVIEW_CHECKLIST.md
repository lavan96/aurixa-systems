# Aurixa Systems legal review checklist

This file is an internal publication gate. Draft wording does not mean that any item below is approved. Aurixa's Australian legal adviser and authorised business stakeholders must approve the final documents before publication.

- [ ] Confirm AURIXA SYSTEMS PTY LTD is the intended contracting entity.
- [ ] Confirm ABN 49 695 868 243.
- [ ] Confirm New South Wales business location and whether a street or postal address must be published.
- [ ] Approve legal and privacy contact email addresses.
- [ ] Approve effective date, last-updated date, version numbers and document status.
- [ ] Confirm Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Australian Privacy Principles and other privacy-law applicability.
- [ ] Confirm minimum user age and intended service audience.
- [ ] Obtain final Australian legal approval.

## Privacy and data operations

- [ ] Map overseas processing countries and applicable safeguards.
- [ ] Confirm all service-provider categories and disclosure language.
- [ ] Approve retention periods by information and record category.
- [ ] Re-audit cookies, analytics, attribution, local storage, session storage, advertising pixels, error monitoring and performance monitoring immediately before publication.
- [ ] Assess platform workflows for legally significant automated decision-making.
- [ ] Confirm incident and privacy complaint processes and required notices.
- [ ] Confirm customer-controlled client and due-diligence data flows.

## Commercial terms and service operations

- [ ] Approve billing cycle, payment methods, automatic renewal and renewal notice.
- [ ] Approve cancellation periods, refund rules, late-payment rules and chargeback handling.
- [ ] Confirm seat limits, credit and usage rules, taxes and GST treatment.
- [ ] Approve trial, demonstration and early-access terms.
- [ ] Approve service levels, maintenance notices and material feature-change notices.
- [ ] Approve termination notice and cure periods.
- [ ] Approve data-export window, post-termination retention, backup deletion and legal-retention exceptions.

## Ownership, risk and agreement mechanics

- [ ] Approve generated-report and deliverable ownership and licensing.
- [ ] Approve the feedback licence.
- [ ] Approve balanced confidentiality wording and survival period.
- [ ] Approve liability cap, excluded losses, scope of responsibility and Australian Consumer Law exceptions.
- [ ] Determine whether any proportionate indemnities are required.
- [ ] Approve New South Wales governing law and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Approve dispute-resolution and mediation process.
- [ ] Approve document precedence, assignment, notices, force majeure and entire-agreement language.
- [ ] Confirm required methods and addresses for formal notices.
